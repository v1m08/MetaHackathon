## 30-second version

"Friendships don't end, they go quiet, and silence has no notification. Facebook is the only app that has everyone you've ever known. Reconnect computes who you've drifted from, in code, from real exchanges. When that friend posts, Muse writes one line tying it to something you actually share, and code checks every citation. A private tap tells you if they miss you too. Then a Muse agent finds a real way to see them, with who's free from what they said and what it costs from a price table, and drafts the message in your voice. You send it. They get a friend's words and an Event with one tap. No chat box, no agent talking to anyone."

## Five hard questions

- **Isn't this just event planning with AI?** Partiful and Doodle start after two people agreed to meet. We work on why nobody asked: the silence is invisible, reaching out feels wrong, and "who's in" dies. The plan is the last ten percent; the nudge, the evidence and the mutual tap are the product.
- **Facebook could ship "you haven't talked to Nadia" without AI.** Yes, and it would be a guilt notification people swipe away. What makes it act-on-able is the model's work: the specific shared thing, the words in your voice, availability read from what people posted. The detection is code on purpose; the reason is Muse.
- **Isn't it creepy?** It reads only what you can already see, only things with both of you in them, never likes, mood, health, or anyone else's chats, and the evidence screen shows you exactly what it used and didn't. The other person never sees anything inferred about them. The heart is private until it's mutual.
- **Prior art?** Hang Again (a prototype on this repo's `reconnect-prototype` branch) had the drift math and the why-now idea; we took both, ran them on the real clone, added the mutual layer, and put the travel engine's planning, availability and validation under the plan.
- **What's simulated?** The people and prices, and when the trigger fires (on first load of the day, not when the friend posts). Every Muse call, every check and both screens are real.
