# Submission — Reconnect

**One line.** Friendships don't end, they go quiet. Facebook is the only place that still knows everyone you've lost touch with. Reconnect hears the silence, tells you when it's mutual, and gives you the reason, the words and a plan to break it.

**Why young adults, and why Facebook.** Young adults have accounts; they don't open them, because the feed is where nobody they know posts anymore. What Facebook still uniquely holds is the past: everyone they've ever known, with fifteen years of tagged photos, events and hometown groups attached, plus Messenger and Events to act on it. Reactivation spreads person to person: every plan here puts one dormant friend back on Facebook with a message from someone they know, not a promotion.

**The unsolved problem.** Event planning is solved for people who already agreed to meet. Three things before that are not: silence has no notification; reaching out after silence feels wrong; and "who's in?" dies unanswered. Reconnect works on those three. The plan is the payoff, not the premise.

**What it does, on surfaces that already exist.** (1) A notification when a drifted friend posts: one line from Muse tying the post to something you actually share. (2) Under the post, the line with its evidence as chips and a **What was used** screen: cited, shared-but-unused, and the list of things never read (likes, mood, health, anyone else's chats). (2b) **I miss them**: a private tap; if they tapped you too, you both find out, and nobody else ever does. (3) **Plan something**: a Muse agent proposes 2–3 real shapes (visit their city, hang out here, an event one of you is already going to, a call) with who, when (each person's own words about the dates), and cost line by line. (4) A draft in your voice in the normal Messenger composer; sending it creates an invite-only Event. (5) The other person gets a friend's words and an Event card with **I'm in**; nothing AI-authored on their side. (6) The plan's home is the Event; **How this was planned** lists every claim with its source; the owner confirms last. (7) Friends → Reconnect: the drift list in four kinds: a person, a chapter (a crew whose ask died), your culture (a group's event friends are going to), a place (a city with quiet friends and a friend driving there).

**How Muse is used.** `muse-spark-1.3` writes the why-now line with evidence ids, runs the gathering agent (a tool-calling loop: context → infer availability → estimate cost → propose) and drafts the message from the user's own past texts; `muse-ember-1.0` extracts dated availability statements with quotes from posts and chats. One client with tracing (the in-app Inspector shows every prompt, step, token and cost), caching and a hard budget guard.

**Safety by construction.** Drift is computed in code from real exchanges; likes never count. Every evidence id a nudge cites must exist, include both people and not be the trigger, or the nudge is dropped; a banned-word list (mood, health, guilt) is enforced in code. Every person, date, event and ride in a plan is re-checked; every dollar is recomputed; model-written prices are stripped. The other person sees nothing inferred about them. Private picks are returned only to their owner, plus a `mutual` bit. The agent never sends a message.

**Outcome, in our seeded evaluation (`EVAL-RECONNECT.md`).** Why-now: 9 drifted pairs (8 viewers), 9 nudges shown, 0 dropped, 9/9 with every citation real and shared, 0/9 with a mood/health/guilt word. Plans: 4 targets (a person, a five-person crew, two cross-city pairs), 12 plans, 12/12 with every person, event and ride id real and visible, 12/12 with the per-person cost equal to its code-computed lines, 0 model-written dollars; average agent latency 51 s, $0.46 of Muse for the whole section. Reactivation, code only, whole seed: 250 friendships, 154 asleep (62%), 38 of them with shared history a nudge can cite, 111 with a post in the last two weeks. Tests 30/30 on code paths; the full two-person path passes 28 automated checks.

**What's real / simulated.** Real: every Muse call, the checks, both screens, the live two-window demo. Seeded: people, chats, posts, events, prices, and three private picks so the mutual moment can happen on stage. Simulated: when the trigger fires (on first load of the day rather than at the friend's post), and message delivery (inside the clone).

**Prior art.** Hang Again (an earlier prototype on this repo's `reconnect-prototype` branch) had the drift math and the why-now idea; this build runs them on the real clone, adds the mutual layer, and puts the travel engine's planning, availability inference and validation under the plan.
